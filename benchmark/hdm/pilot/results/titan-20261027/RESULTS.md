# Post-selection coverage pilot — DESCRIPTIVE

Defaults are unchanged. This pilot is a descriptive screen, not a validation of coverage.
Observed 180000 / 180000 planned arm records; all denominators retain 500 datasets per case.
Missing chunks are unavailable, not successful fits. Conditional rates exclude invalid fits.
Pooled rates average valid-role means within datasets, then average datasets with a valid role.
Their 95% normal bounds use dataset MCSE; a single dataset gives no MCSE. Single-role Wilson intervals are in summary.csv.

DESCRIPTIVE flags, in order: availability ≥99% for every nonempty pooled group; active-coverage lower 95% bound ≥92.5%; null-rejection upper 95% bound ≤7.5%.
NA means no applicable roles or insufficient datasets for uncertainty. No multiplicity adjustment is made.
