# Preliminary 20-dataset development findings

EMPIRICAL. These are development results, not fresh confirmation. There are 19 designs, 380 response datasets, 48,574 distinct target records including derived HC1 comparisons. Dataset-level Monte Carlo errors are in summary.json. All input completion markers were present. There were 161 method failure receipts and 223 warning files; these remain in the report. A completion marker records attempts, not reliable inference. No production default is changed.

The most promising broad Gaussian candidate in this pilot is the regularized initializer plus restricted LDPE and robust HC1 variance. Active coverage ranges from .92 to .99 over the nine Gaussian designs. With the same projection policy, simple selected-model refitting gives .74 in the moderate design and .85 in the random-support design, even after HC1. This contradicts using naive post-selection refitting as a universal default. Correctly chosen refitting or double selection remains worth studying.

For logistic regression, no tested method is calibrated in every design. The current restricted-LDPE one-step rule has .89 active coverage in the moderate case. Native SIHR, hdm, and CV double selection also have failures or undercoverage in some designs. Published/package methods do provide positive controls, including the Belloni and SIHR-style designs and larger-sample cases, but those controls do not establish reliability on every hard design. The 20-replication estimates are noisy, especially Belloni's single active target.

The native hdm Gaussian multi-target double-selection wrapper can fail on NULL selection metadata. The unchanged single-target public API avoids that wrapper path. Ninety independently constructed FWL/sandwich comparisons agreed within 1e-8. Both results are retained separately. Xia's refined inverse-Hessian implementation is used only for p<n designs; its original source and provenance remain in the run registry.

The half-universal QP constraint is genuinely infeasible in two of three checked correlated datasets. Two numerical solvers and explicit null-space dual vectors support this finding; floating-point residuals were about 1e-16. Inaccurate solver statuses are preserved. Lowering every QP penalty is not a valid general repair. Numerical feasibility also does not imply confidence-interval calibration.

Next development checks expand the same seed namespace to 100 datasets per design and investigate fully solved target/intercept score equations with regularized, half-relaxed, and one-standard-error-selected/refitted nuisances. These are distinct experimental estimators. The 92200000 confirmation namespace remains untouched. Freeze any chosen rule before fresh validation; do not select rules on confirmation results. All fits and aggregation run on Discovery. Timing here includes compilation/shared diagnostic work and is not a competitor speed comparison.
