StatsAPI.coef — Method
coef(sol::GLMLassoSolution)
predict(sol::GLMLassoSolution, X; type = :response, offset = nothing)
fitted(sol::GLMLassoSolution)

The coefficients [β₀; β] (β without an intercept), as a SparseVector. The predictions at the rows of X, on the scale of the response (type = :response, the probabilities of logistic regression and the means of Poisson regression) or of the linear predictor β₀ + Xβ + offset (type = :link). fitted is the response scale at the data of the fit. nobs, dof (nonzero coefficients, plus one for the intercept), deviance and nulldeviance apply too.

StatsAPI.coef — Method
coef(sol::MultiLassoSolution)

The p × q coefficients B as a sparse matrix, with the intercepts of the responses in a first row when the solver fitted them, and the n × q fitted values 1β₀' + XB at the rows of X. nobs and dof (the nonzero coefficients, plus one intercept per response) apply too.

StatsAPI.coef — Method
coef(r::PostLassoResult)
stderror(r::PostLassoResult)
vcov(r::PostLassoResult)
confint(r::PostLassoResult; level = 0.95)

The least-squares coefficients [β₀; β] (β without an intercept) and their HC1 standard errors as SparseVectors, zero off the selected columns, and their covariance as a sparse matrix. confint gives normal intervals coef ± z stderror at level, as a matrix with a row per coefficient; those off the selected columns are [0, 0], since the coefficients were not estimated. predict, residuals, nobs, dof (the coefficients estimated) and deviance (the residual sum of squares) apply too.

StatsAPI.coef — Method
coef(path::LassoPath)
coef(path::LassoPath, λ)

The coefficients along the path, as a sparse matrix with one column per value of path.λpath, whose first row holds the intercepts when the path has an intercept. With λ, the coefficients at λ as a SparseVector, interpolated linearly in λ between the two neighboring values of λpath (as glmnet's coef(fit, s = λ)), and exact at the values of λpath; λ must be within the range of λpath.

StatsAPI.deviance — Method
deviance(sol::LassoSolution)

The residual sum of squares ‖y - β₀ - X * β‖², weighted, ∑ᵢ wᵢ rᵢ², for a fit with observation weights.

source
StatsAPI.dof — Method
dof(sol::LassoSolution)

The number of nonzero coefficients, plus one for an intercept: an unbiased estimate of the degrees of freedom of the lasso (Zou, Hastie and Tibshirani, On the "degrees of freedom" of the lasso, 2007). For the elastic net, MCP and SCAD it is the count that glmnet and ncvreg report, not an unbiased estimate.

The residuals y - β₀ - X * β of the data the solver was given. A solution computed from sufficient statistics (scaled_lasso! with a CDSqrtQuadraticLoss) has none, and an error is thrown; so it is for nobs and deviance.

HighDimensionalModels.FormulaFit — Type
FormulaFit

A model fitted from a formula and a table. Loading StatsModels.jl adds formula methods to the estimators, which return it:

using HighDimensionalModels, StatsModels
path = fit(LassoPath, @formula(y ~ x1 + x2 + log(x3) + region), data)
sol = lasso(@formula(y ~ x1 + x2 + region), data, 0.1; standardize_x = true)
grp = group_lasso(@formula(y ~ x1 + region + x2 & region), data, 0.05)   # a group per term

The formula methods are fit with LassoPath, CVLassoPath, GLMLassoPath, CVGLMLassoPath, GroupLassoPath and SparseGroupLassoPath, and lasso, sqrt_lasso, adaptive_lasso, glm_lasso, group_lasso, sparse_group_lasso and rlasso with the formula and the table in place of X and y. The other arguments and keywords are those of the estimator, with these differences:

  • The formula sets the intercept: y ~ x1 + x2 fits one, y ~ 0 + x1 + x2 does not, and the keyword intercept is an error.
  • Categorical variables (strings, or CategoricalArrays) become dummy columns, coded by contrasts, a Dict from variable names to StatsModels contrasts. The lasso penalizes each dummy column; the group lasso and the sparse group lasso take the columns of each term as a group unless groups is given.
  • weights and offset, where the estimator takes them, may also be names of columns of the table.
  • Rows with a missing value in a variable of the model are left out.

model is the fitted model, a LassoPath, LassoSolution and so on, and formula the formula with the schema of the data applied. coef, nobs, dof, deviance, fitted, residuals, stderror, vcov and confint forward to the model, with their arguments: coef(m, λ) for a path. coefnames(m) names the coefficients in the order of coef(m), the intercept first, and responsename(m) the response. predict(m, newdata; kwargs...) builds the columns of the table newdata by the formula and predicts with the model.
