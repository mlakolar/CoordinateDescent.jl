Solutions and StatsAPI

CVMultiLassoPath supports coef and predict at λ1se or λmin. DirtyMultitaskSolution returns total coefficients and a canonical component split, with a recomputed kkt residual and a relative duality gap. Dirty CV uses its minimum-error (ratio, λ_specific) fit; it has no 1se rule.

StatsAPI.coef — Method
coef(cv::CVMultiLassoPath; λ = :λ1se)
predict(cv::CVMultiLassoPath, X; λ = :λ1se)

The coefficients (with intercepts in the first row when fitted), and the matrix of predictions at the rows of X, at cv.λ1se, at cv.λmin with λ = :λmin, or at a level within the path.

HighDimensionalModels.FormulaFit — Type
FormulaFit

A model fitted from a formula and a table. Loading StatsModels.jl adds formula methods to the estimators, which return it:

using HighDimensionalModels, StatsModels
path = fit(LassoPath, @formula(y ~ x1 + x2 + log(x3) + region), data)
sol = lasso(@formula(y ~ x1 + x2 + region), data, 0.1; standardize_x = true)
grp = group_lasso(@formula(y ~ x1 + region + x2 & region), data, 0.05)   # a group per term

The formula methods are fit with LassoPath, CVLassoPath, GLMLassoPath, CVGLMLassoPath, GroupLassoPath and SparseGroupLassoPath, and lasso, sqrt_lasso, adaptive_lasso, glm_lasso, group_lasso, sparse_group_lasso and rlasso with the formula and the table in place of X and y. The other arguments and keywords are those of the estimator, with these differences:

  • The formula sets the intercept: y ~ x1 + x2 fits one, y ~ 0 + x1 + x2 does not, and the keyword intercept is an error.
  • Categorical variables (strings, or CategoricalArrays) become dummy columns, coded by contrasts, a Dict from variable names to StatsModels contrasts. The lasso penalizes each dummy column; the group lasso and the sparse group lasso take the columns of each term as a group unless groups is given.
  • weights and offset, where the estimator takes them, may also be names of columns of the table.
  • Rows with a missing value in a variable of the model are left out.

model is the fitted model, a LassoPath, LassoSolution and so on, and formula the formula with the schema of the data applied. coef, nobs, dof, deviance, fitted, residuals, stderror, vcov and confint forward to the model, with their arguments: coef(m, λ) for a path. coefnames(m) names the coefficients in the order of coef(m), the intercept first, and responsename(m) the response. predict(m, newdata; kwargs...) builds the columns of the table newdata by the formula and predicts with the model.

HighDimensionalModels.DirtyMultitaskSolution — Type
DirtyMultitaskSolution

The dirty multitask fit of dirty_multitask. B, A and S are sparse p × q matrices on the data scale, with B = A + S. intercept holds the task intercepts, zero without hasintercept. λ_shared, λ_specific, shared_norm, task_weights and penalty_factors record the objective. activerows are the nonzero rows of B; sharedrows are those of the canonical split A, not identified shared features.

objective is the two-component objective. gap is the relative duality gap, or NaN when unavailable. kkt is the largest normalized violation of dual feasibility, the two Fenchel equalities, intercept stationarity, B = A + S and zeros outside the fitted coordinates, recomputed at the returned point. Each residual is divided by the maximum of one and the magnitudes of its terms. iterations counts row passes; converged requires the change rule and any requested, available gap target. nobs = sum(task_nobs) counts all tasks' observations, unlike multi_lasso's observation count. dof counts nonzero entries of B plus intercepts; it is not effective degrees of freedom.

source

HighDimensionalModels.SparseNuclearSolution — Type
SparseNuclearSolution

A fit of ‖Y - 1β₀' - XB‖²_F/(2n) + λ_rank ‖B‖_* + λ_sparse P(B). B, intercept, residuals, objective, activerows and nnz describe the returned coefficients. weights are positive row weights; sparsity is :entry or :row. rank counts singular values above the reported rank_tol, which also accounts for disagreement with the nuclear proximal point. gap is the relative duality gap, NaN for unpenalized least squares. iterations and converged report solver termination; r_norm, s_norm, eps_pri and eps_dual retain its KKT residuals and tolerances. coef, predict, residuals, nobs and deviance apply. No degrees of freedom or information criterion is provided for this estimator.

source
HighDimensionalModels.LowRankPlusSparseSolution — Type
LowRankPlusSparseSolution

A fit of ‖Y - 1β₀' - X(L + S)‖²_F/(2n) + λ_rank ‖L‖_* + λ_sparse P(S). L, S and B = L + S are independent matrices. intercept, residuals, objective, coef and predict use the total B. weights are positive predictor weights, and sparsity is :entry or :row.

rank counts singular values of B above rank_tol = √eps(T) σ₁(B). lowrank_rank is the rank kept by the last nuclear proximal step (the numerical rank of L on a zero-penalty axis). sparse_nnz and sparse_rows describe the exact support of S. gap is the relative intersection-dual gap, up to floating-point accuracy. lowrank_residual and sparse_residual are Frobenius norms of the component proximal-gradient mappings at the returned pair. iterations and converged describe termination. On either zero-penalty axis, a minimum-norm least-squares convention applies and gap = NaN.

Fitted values are unique; components need not be. A small gap bounds objective error, not component error. coef, predict, residuals, nobs and deviance apply. No degrees of freedom or information criterion is provided for this estimator.
